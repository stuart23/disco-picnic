While we're all stuck at home and cannot go out to see the world, maybe we can bring a bit of the world back to our dining tables.

Let's go on a world tour without breaking any quarantine rules. To do this, we need each person to pick their favourite place in the world and cook a dish that best represents that place. Let’s see your paella from Pamplona, your dahl from Dehli, or your omakase from Osaka.

The contest:

There are two categories for judging, and a winner will be awarded for each category. The categories are as follows:

Gastronomy:

Each person is to bring a dish that represents their chosen favourite place. Dishes will be judged on effort and technical skills, presentation, overt use of adjectives to describe the dishes elements, and taste based on your reaction to tasting it.

Ambience:

The environment is as important to the experience as the food itself. A costume and props that represents your chosen location will put you in the forefront of winning this category. As we will be on Zoom, choosing an appropriate background image to complement your theme will be paramount.
